This is a translation, provided so you can read in English what you are agreeing to. The Spanish version is the legally binding one: Wayfare is run from Spain and governed by Spanish law, so if the two ever disagree, the Spanish text prevails.

What happens in your browser (and stays there)

  • Your questionnaire answers (nationality, age, education…) and your map are stored only in your browser's local storage. They never travel to any server, we cannot see them, and they disappear if you clear the site's data.
  • There are no user accounts, no passwords and no personal identifiers.
  • We use no cookies — neither our own nor third-party ones. That is why you will not see a cookie banner: there is nothing to accept.

Cookie-free statistics (GoatCounter)

  • To know how many people visit us we use GoatCounter, a cookie-free analytics service that builds no profiles and does not track you across sites. It records visits in aggregate (page viewed, approximate country, browser type) without storing your IP address in an identifiable way.
  • The «events» we measure (for example, how many users complete the questionnaire or press share) are anonymous counters: they say how many, never who.

The review form (the only personal data)

  • If you leave a review of an adviser, the name you type and the text of your review are emailed to the site owner through Formsubmit, who moderates it by hand before publishing. Legal basis: your consent when you press send.
  • The published review shows the name exactly as you wrote it. You can ask for it to be corrected or deleted at any time by writing to contacto@[DOMINIO-PENDIENTE].
  • Please do not use the form to send sensitive data (passport numbers, case files…): we do not need it and we will delete it without publishing it.

Email notices (only if you ask for them)

  • If you ask to be told about changes to a visa, your email address does not reach Wayfare: it goes straight to the newsletter service that runs the list. Legal basis: your consent, given by ticking the box.
  • Double confirmation. You will get an email asking you to confirm. Until you click it you are not subscribed. This stops anyone signing up somebody else's address.
  • We only write when one of the official sources we track changes: a rule, a requirement, or a quota opening. No third-party advertising.
  • Every email carries a one-click unsubscribe link. You can also ask by writing to the contact address.
  • We keep your address for as long as you stay subscribed. If you unsubscribe, it is deleted from the list.

Technical third parties (full transparency)

  • GitHub Pages hosts this site: like any web server, GitHub sees the IP address of whoever visits (their policy).
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces: when they load, Google sees your IP (their policy).
  • GoatCounter loads its counter from gc.zgo.at (see the statistics section).
  • Links to advisers carry a tag (utm_source=wayfare) that only tells the adviser you came from Wayfare — it carries no data about you.

Your rights (GDPR)

  • You can exercise access, rectification, erasure, objection and portability by writing to contacto@[DOMINIO-PENDIENTE]. We reply as soon as we can and always within the legal deadline of one month.
  • If you believe we have not handled your data properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency.
  • Changes to this policy are published on this same page, with the last-updated date at the top.
